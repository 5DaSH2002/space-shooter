📖 HOW TO PLAY

Pilot your ship across 25 space sectors. Destroy enemy waves, collect power-ups, and defeat each sector’s unique boss.

CONTROLS

Virtual joystick or A/D/W/S / arrow keys — move freely in any direction

Hold Space or the FIRE button to shoot. Each weapon type has its own sound & look.

P key or the Pause button — pause the game. Use 🌐 to switch English / Arabic.

POWER-UPS (pick up the floating shapes)

⚡ Yellow lightning — Rapid Fire (much faster shooting)

💧 Cyan hex — Barrier +5 (blocks the next 5 hits)

🌀 Magenta spiked orb — Damage Up (stronger bullets)

❄ Three cyan diamonds — Triple Shot (3 bullets at once)

🛸 Mini green ship — Wingman drone fights beside you

🚀 Orange rocket — Rocket Barrage (explosive splash)

🟡 Twin yellow capsules — Dual Laser (2 high-speed beams)

🔴 Red crystal beam — Death Beam (hold FIRE to melt a column)

🔵 Ice crystal — Frost Shot (piercing icy bolts)

🟢 Green star — Nova Burst (shots explode in an area)

🪙 Coins buy ships & upgrades in the Shop. Weapon power-ups replace each other until they expire, then your base weapon returns.
