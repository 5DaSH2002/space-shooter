📖 HOW TO PLAY

Destroy enemies, dodge enemy fire, and beat the boss to clear each of the 10 sectors.

CONTROLS

⬅️ ➡️ or A / D — move your ship (or hold the ◀ ▶ buttons)

Space bar or the FIRE button — shoot

P key or the Pause button — pause the game

POWER-UPS

⚡ Rapid Fire — shoot much faster for a short time

💧 Barrier +5 — a shield that blocks the next 5 enemy hits

🌀 Damage Up — your bullets hit harder for a while

❄ Triple Shot — fire 3 bullets at once instead of 1

🛸 Wingman — a drone escort fights alongside you

🪙 Collect coins to buy new ships & upgrades in the Shop
